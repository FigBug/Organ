Organ by SocaLabs
==================

This installer places the Organ plug-in in your system audio plug-in
folders along with the factory presets.

Components:
  • VST                  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST
  • VST3                 /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit           /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • CLAP                 /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP
  • Factory presets      /Library/Audio/Presets/SocaLabs/Organ

User-saved presets live under
  ~/Library/Audio/Presets/SocaLabs/Organ

and are not touched by this installer.
